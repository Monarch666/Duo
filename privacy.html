Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

1. Introduction

Duo Sanctuary ("Duo", "we", "our") is designed from the ground up to protect the intimacy and privacy of exactly two partners. We believe your relationship data should remain strictly yours.

2. Zero Third-Party Servers (Bring-Your-Own-Cloud)

Unlike traditional apps, Duo operates on a decentralized Bring-Your-Own-Cloud (BYOC) architecture. We do not host your relationship data, notes, bucket lists, calendar events, or photos on centralized developer servers. All data is stored directly on your personal device and optionally synchronized with your own personal Google Drive account.

3. Google User Data & OAuth Scopes

When you choose to sign in with Google or enable Google Drive Sync, Duo requests the following scopes:

  • Basic Profile & Email: Used solely to identify you and your partner within your private sanctuary space.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file: Used solely to create and manage an isolated Duo Sanctuary folder in your own personal Google Drive storage. Duo only accesses files created by Duo itself; it cannot access, view, or modify any other files in your Google Drive.

4. How We Use and Share Google Data

Google user data and Drive files are used strictly to provide the core synchronization features between you and your partner.

  • We do not sell, rent, or transfer your Google user data to any third parties or advertising networks.
  • We do not use your data for advertising, machine learning training, or commercial profiling.
  • Data transmission occurs exclusively between your device and Google's official API servers via TLS/HTTPS.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because your data is stored in your personal Google Drive and device storage, you retain full ownership. You can delete your data at any time by clearing app data or deleting the Duo Sanctuary folder in your Google Drive.
